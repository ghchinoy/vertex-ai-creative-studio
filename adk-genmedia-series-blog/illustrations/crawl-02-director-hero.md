## Prompt (paste into Photoshoot / `nanobanana_image_generation`)

> Flat editorial vector illustration, warm and tactile, light paper-grain texture, soft long
> shadows, on a warm off-white background. A friendly flat-design **film-director** persona holding
> a **teal** clapperboard, framing a shot with one hand. In front of the director, a short strip of
> film/video frames shows the **same red umbrella tumbling down a rain-slicked neon Tokyo street**
> (continuity with the Photoshoot hero, now in motion). Two diverging paths lead out of the
> clapperboard: the main path, in **indigo** structural lines, leads to a small **green**
> tagged clip labelled with a tiny cloud/`gs://` glyph (a verified, stored video); a secondary,
> **dashed orange** path branches off to a small **orange warning triangle** (the model-gating
> footgun — the naive call that fails). A faint **amber** glow around the director's head signals
> the reasoning that chooses the correct path. Modern developer-blog aesthetic, generous negative
> space, upper-left third clear for a title overlay. No text, no logos, not photoreal, no UI
> screenshots.
