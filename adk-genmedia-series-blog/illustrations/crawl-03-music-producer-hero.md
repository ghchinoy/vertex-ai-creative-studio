## Prompt (paste into Photoshoot / `nanobanana_image_generation`)

> Flat editorial vector illustration, warm and tactile, light paper-grain texture, soft long
> shadows, on a warm off-white background. A friendly flat-design **music producer** persona at a
> small mixing desk, headphones on. Three **teal** instrument/tool icons feed into the desk from the
> left — a music-note/keyboard (music bed), a microphone (voiceover), and a small waveform-merge
> icon (the mix) — each connected to the desk by a clearly **labelled indigo channel strip** (three
> parallel indigo lanes, evoking namespaced tool channels). The three lanes converge into a single
> output waveform on the right that carries a small **green check tag** (a verified mixed file). A
> soft **amber** glow at the producer's hands suggests planning the order of operations. Keep one lane
> subtly marked with a small **orange** dot to hint at a gotcha (a silently-dropped control). Modern
> developer-blog aesthetic, generous negative space, upper-left third clear for a title overlay. No
> text, no logos, not photoreal, no UI screenshots.
