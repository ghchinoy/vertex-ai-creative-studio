## Prompt (paste into Photoshoot / `nanobanana_image_generation`)

> Flat editorial vector illustration, warm and tactile, with a light paper-grain texture and soft
> long shadows, on a warm off-white background. A left-to-right journey composition: three small
> flat-design creator personas walking a gently rising path — a **photographer** holding a camera,
> a **film director** with a clapperboard, and a **music producer** at a small mixing desk — each
> connected by clean indigo blueprint lines that thicken as the path rises. Above the personas, a
> single warm **amber spark/glow** travels along the path, growing brighter left to right,
> representing an idea being refined. The tools each persona holds are rendered in **teal**; the
> connective structure and path in **indigo**; the idea-spark in **amber**. Minimal, modern
> developer-blog aesthetic; generous negative space, especially the upper-left third (kept clear
> for a title overlay). No text, no logos, no photoreal rendering, no UI screenshots.
